About

A longer introduction.

I build things that work well. Not because I obsess over polish for its own sake, but because I believe the quality of what you make reflects the quality of how you think. I care about systems that scale, design that communicates, and products that earn the attention they ask for.

I'm one half of a pair of twins. My brother Albert and I share a birthday, a competitive streak, and an unreasonable belief that most problems are solvable if you just refuse to stop working on them.

Outside of building, I train across ten different sports, which sounds excessive until you realize most of them are just excuses to be outside and moving. I believe the body and the mind are the same system and you can't sharpen one while neglecting the other.

How I think

  • 01 Discipline is a form of freedom. Constraints create clarity. The more decisions you automate, the more willpower you have for the ones that actually matter.
  • 02 Speed comes from smoothness. Rushing produces friction. The fastest path is the one with the fewest wasted motions.
  • 03 Build before you talk. The gap between ideas and execution is where most things die. Ship something, then iterate. Talk is cheap and empty barrels make the most noise.
  • 04 Compound quietly. The things that matter most are rarely visible day-to-day. Show up consistently, and the results will speak louder than any announcement.
  • 05 Rest is a strategy, not a reward. Sleep is the most powerful drug. Recovery is part of the system, not a break from it.
